# Accessibility Requirements

WCAG 2.2 AA is the baseline. ALSense must work without a mouse, without reliable speech, and without completing a long task in one sitting.

- Full keyboard and switch navigation with visible focus and logical order.
- Semantic labels, status announcements, error summaries, and screen-reader-tested workflows.
- Large configurable targets, reflow at high zoom, adjustable text, strong contrast, reduced motion, and no drag-only actions.
- Save/resume, no avoidable time limits, short steps, low typing burden, and fatigue-aware defaults.
- Equivalent text for audio and equivalent non-speech controls for voice features.
- Support for eye gaze, switch control, on-screen keyboards, and caregiver-assisted entry.
- Record who entered data and allow patient review/revocation of caregiver access.
- Never use speech quality, motor speed, or interaction difficulty as evidence of cognition or consent capacity.
- Backend APIs must support drafts, idempotent submissions, partial updates, correction history, and preference profiles so accessibility is not dependent on a future frontend retrofit.
